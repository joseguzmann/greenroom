# Greenroom

A practice room for the conversations that matter. You walk in, an AI agent plays the other side
by voice, and afterwards you get feedback grounded in what you actually said and a plan for what to
work on next.

The first room is a **mock interviewer**: you join a call, the interviewer runs the screen the way
the AI interviews companies send today do, follows up on your answers, and the debrief scores you
against a rubric.

Built as a multi-agent system: an analyst prepares the session from the CV and the job posting,
the interviewer runs it live by voice, an evaluator scores it against a rubric, and a coach turns
the gaps into study work.
