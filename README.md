# ai-interviewer

A voice AI interviewer for practicing job interviews: you join a call, an AI interviewer runs the
screen the way the AI interviews companies send today do, and afterwards you get an evaluation
grounded in what you actually said and a plan for what to study next.

Built as a multi-agent system: an analyst prepares the interview from the CV and the job posting,
the interviewer runs it live by voice, an evaluator scores it against a rubric, and a coach turns
the gaps into study work.
